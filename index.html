Privacy Policy (English)

Fire Rush ("the Game", Android package com.firerush.game) is a single-player arcade shoot-'em-up published by an independent developer. We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal data ourselves. The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, and AdMob collects certain data as described below.

Data we collect ourselves

None. There is no account system and no login. Your game progress (unlocked stages, energy, upgrades, best waves, language and sound settings) is saved only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If Android Auto Backup is enabled, that save may be included in your own Google account backup, controlled entirely by Google and your device settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your advertising identifier (Advertising ID), device and app information, coarse/approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction events. This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and its AdMob data disclosures. You can reset or limit ad personalization via your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Permissions

The Game requests: INTERNET (to load ads), VIBRATE (haptic feedback), and the advertising-ID permission used by the ads SDK. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are served in accordance with the applicable AdMob/Play family policies for the app's target audience.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update it here and disclose the change in the store listing before releasing the new version.

Contact

隐私政策（中文）

Fire Rush（异星突围，Android 包名 com.firerush.game）是一款由独立开发者发布的 单人街机射击游戏。我们没有自建服务器，也不会由我们自己收集你的个人信息。游戏通过 Google AdMob 展示广告，AdMob 会按下述方式收集部分数据。

我们自己收集的数据

不收集。没有账号系统、没有登录。你的游戏进度（已解锁关卡、能量、强化、最高 波次、语言与声音设置）仅通过本地存储保存在你自己的设备上，不会离开你的设备， 我们无法读取，卸载游戏或清除数据后即被删除。如果你开启了 Android 自动备份，该存档可能被纳入你 自己 Google 账号的备份，完全由 Google 和你的设备设置控制。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏通过 Google AdMob 展示激励视频和插屏广告。为投放与统计广告，Google 可能收集并处理如下数据：你的广告标识符（Advertising ID）、设备与应用信息、 由 IP 推断的粗略位置、以及广告交互事件。这些数据由 Google（而非我们）收集，受 Google 隐私政策 与其 AdMob 数据披露 约束。你可在设备设置中重置或限制广告个性化（设置 → Google → 广告）。

权限

游戏申请：网络（INTERNET）（用于加载广告）、震动（VIBRATE） （触感反馈），以及广告 SDK 使用的广告 ID 权限。不申请定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录或存储。

儿童

本游戏并非面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集其数据。广告将按应用目标受众适用的 AdMob/Play 家庭政策进行投放。

变更

若本政策发生重大变更，我们会在此更新，并在发布新版本前于商店信息中披露。
