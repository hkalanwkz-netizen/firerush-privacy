Privacy Policy (English)

Fire Rush ("the Game", Android package com.firerush.game) is a single-player, offline arcade shoot-'em-up published by an independent developer. This version is designed to collect no personal data at all.

What we collect

Nothing. The Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. There is no account system, no login, no advertising SDK, and no analytics SDK in this version.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked stages, energy, upgrades, best waves, language and sound settings) is saved only on your device using the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If you have Android Auto Backup enabled, Android may include this local save in your own Google account backup; that backup is controlled entirely by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Permissions

The Game requests a single permission: VIBRATE, used for haptic feedback during gameplay. It does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Network & children

The Game runs fully offline and makes no network requests. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds data collection (for example, ads or online features), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be disclosed in the store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hkalanwkz@gmail.com

隐私政策（中文）

Fire Rush（异星突围，Android 包名 com.firerush.game）是一款由独立开发者发布的 单人离线街机射击游戏。此版本的设计目标是完全不收集任何个人信息。

我们收集哪些信息

不收集任何信息。本游戏不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息或敏感信息。此版本没有 账号系统、没有登录、没有广告 SDK、也没有统计分析 SDK。

存储在你设备上的数据

你的游戏进度（已解锁关卡、能量、强化、最高波次、语言与声音设置）仅通过应用本地存储保存在 你自己的设备上。这些数据不会离开你的设备，我们和任何第三方都无法读取，卸载游戏 或清除应用数据后即被删除。如果你开启了 Android 自动备份，系统可能会把该本地存档纳入你自己 Google 账号的备份中；该备份完全由 Google 和你的设备设置控制，与我们无关。

权限

本游戏仅申请一项权限：震动（VIBRATE），用于游戏中的触感反馈。本游戏不申请 网络、定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录、存储或任何其他敏感权限。

网络与儿童

本游戏完全离线运行，不发起任何网络请求，也不会主动收集任何人（包括儿童）的数据。

变更

若未来版本新增数据收集（例如广告或联网功能），我们会在该版本发布前更新本政策，并在商店信息中 披露相关变更。
